Frequently Asked Questions

What is a raised storage area?

It is an elevated platform that increases storage capacity by taking advantage of unused overhead space. Raised storage areas are ideal for warehouses or facilities with limited floor space.

What applications can raised storage areas and mezzanine offices serve?

These systems can be configured as raised storage, production areas, office areas, clean rooms, lunch rooms or even onsite gyms.

What support services do you provide?

Dynamic offers layout and design services from your photos and sketches, recommended site consultations, a 25-year structural warranty, and a mandatory pre-fabrication site inspection to ensure absolute accuracy.

What materials and features are used in raised storage areas?

The platforms are built from high‑quality materials such as durable steel frameworks and sturdy decking. Solutions include customisable design options, strong construction and seamless integration into warehouse or office environments.

Are raised storage areas compliant with safety standards?

Yes. Raised storage solutions are engineered to comply with Australian standards, and Dynamic provides warranty and quality assurance to ensure long‑term safety.

What clear height is required for a raised storage area or mezzanine office?

To comply with building regulations, you need a minimum headroom clearance of 2100mm both above and below the mezzanine floor. Since the steel deck structure has a minimum depth of 250mm, a clear warehouse roof height of 4450mm or greater is necessary.
