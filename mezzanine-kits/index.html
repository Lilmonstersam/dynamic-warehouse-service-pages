Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a mezzanine floor kit?

Kits comprise prefabricated steel beams, decking panels and fixings, along with optional stairs and safety gates. They are delivered ready for assembly and can be configured to suit your space.

What sizes and load capacities are available?

Dynamic offers light-duty kits (3kPa / 300kg/m²), medium/heavy-duty kits (5kPa / 500kg/m²), and custom kit designs for weight capacities up to 1000kg/m².

Do the kits need to be fixed to walls or floors?

No. Our mezzanine kits are freestanding, meaning they do not require attachment to the building’s walls or main structure. They are, however, securely bolted to the concrete floor slab to ensure structural stability and regulatory compliance. This allows for straightforward installation and future relocation.

Are mezzanine kits compliant with Australian standards?

Yes. Kits are designed and fabricated in Australia, comply with Australian standards and BCA codes, and offer high safety standards.

What are the typical uses for mezzanine floor kits?

Kits can be used in warehouses, factories, retail spaces and offices to increase storage or workspace. They are customisable, modular and reusable, with optional additions such as staircases, pallet access gates, barricades and walkways.

How long does installation take and what does it cost?

Kits have short lead times: most are fabricated and installed within 2 to 3 weeks, depending on workshop capacity. Our installation crews work quickly, fitting between 50 and 100 square metres per day. Pricing varies with size and load rating, generally ranging from $20,000 to over $100,000.

What ceiling height is required for a mezzanine kit?

A minimum roof height of 4450mm is needed to ensure the required 2100mm headroom above and below the deck, accounting for a minimum floor structure thickness of 250mm.
