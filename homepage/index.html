Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mezzanine floor?

Typically, mezzanine floors are used to provide raised storage space, office space, production areas, workrooms, canteens etc but can be used for anything that requires additional floor space.

Are standard mezzanine sizes available?

Yes, we manufacture and stock a range of standard beam spans, column heights and stairs. These are prefabricated and finished in an attractive charcoal painted finish. This ensures faster turnaround as products are in stock and a more economical mezzanine solution compared to a full customised mezzanine. We can design a mezzanine based on our standard sizes to suit your exact requirements.

What permits do I need when planning the mezzanine?

Mezzanine floors range from small freestanding removable storage structures which only require engineering certification to large scale mezzanine office areas which require planning and building permits. We can guide you on what is required and as we are registered commercial builders we can arrange any required permits for you as part of our turn-key solution.

How high must the ceiling be for a mezzanine?

Our standard finished floor heights (using our Hi-Span system) are 2580, 3000 and 3400mm. Our mezzanines can be manufactured to any floor height to best suit your requirements. Although, keep in mind that any floor height over 3400mm will require an additional landing in staircase to comply with Australian building codes and regulations.
