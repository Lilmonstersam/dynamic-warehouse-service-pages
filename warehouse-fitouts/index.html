Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Dynamic provide for warehouse fit‑outs?

Dynamic’s fit‑out division handles everything from concept to completion, including walls and partitioning, electrical/data/lighting, mezzanine floors, floor coverings, shelving and storage, pallet storage solutions, painting, permit documentation, plumbing, mechanical, line marking and warehouse equipment.

Why choose Dynamic for a warehouse fit‑out?

With over 20 years’ experience in the warehouse storage industry, the company combines space designers, sales consultants, project managers and licensed tradespeople to deliver safe and productive workspaces on time and within budget.

Do you offer custom solutions?

Yes. We provide free design and layout proposals, which can be initiated from drawings or photos. We recommend a free site visit to assess your layout, and a final pre-fabrication site inspection is mandatory.

Is health and safety considered during the fit‑out?

Absolutely. Dynamic emphasises the importance of efficient, presentable and safe work environments. The team works with clients to ensure all fit‑outs meet safety standards and create productive workplaces.

What clear height is required for warehouse fit-outs involving mezzanines?

A minimum headroom of 2100mm is required both above and below the mezzanine floor. With minimum floor structural depth taking 250mm, a clear roof height of at least 4450mm is required.
