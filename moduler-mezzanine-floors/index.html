Frequently Asked Questions

What is a modular mezzanine floor?

A modular mezzanine is a prefabricated steel platform that is assembled on‑site. Dynamic’s Hi‑Span system is pre‑engineered and designed to comply with Australian Standards, offering quick installation and the ability to relocate when business needs change.

What are the advantages of a modular mezzanine?

Modular mezzanines are pre‑engineered for full compliance and don’t require new foundations. Components and kits are held in stock for quick turnaround. They can be expanded or relocated easily, making them ideal for growing businesses.

Can modular mezzanines be customised?

Yes. You can tailor the size, height and load rating of a modular mezzanine; accessories such as stairs, gates and balustrades can also be added.

What industries use modular mezzanines?

These systems are suited to warehouses, factories, workshops, retail outlets and hospitality spaces. Prefabricated mezzanine kits are freestanding and do not rely on the existing building structure, offering flexibility for many sectors.

Are modular mezzanines compliant with Australian standards?

Yes. The Hi‑Span system meets Australian building and safety codes, with engineer‑certified designs.

What roof height do I need for a modular mezzanine?

For a compliant space, you need a minimum headroom clearance of 2100mm above and below the floor. Including the 250mm structure depth, a roof height of at least 4450mm is required.

Is an initial site visit necessary for a modular quote?

No. We can draft a proposal and estimate based on drawings or photos. We recommend a free site visit to confirm details, and a final site inspection before fabrication is mandatory.

How quickly can a modular mezzanine be installed?

Modular components are stocked for fast turnaround. Lead times are typically 2 to 3 weeks, and our installation crews fit between 50 and 100 square metres per day, meaning most builds are completed in just a few days.
