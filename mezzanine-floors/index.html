Mezzanine Types

Five ways to add floor space, from an in-stock modular system through to a full warehouse fit-out.

Modular Mezzanine Floors

A prefabricated steel platform assembled on site, pre-engineered to comply with Australian Standards. Suits warehouses, factories, workshops, large retail and hospitality where you need more area without the cost and disruption of a traditional renovation.

Learn more

Custom Mezzanine Floors

Designed to match specific site conditions, sizes and shapes. The right call for complex sites, clean rooms, unusual load requirements, or anywhere a standard system will not fit.

Learn more

Mezzanine Floor Kits

Prefabricated steel beams, decking panels and fixings supplied as a customisable kit. Suits warehouses, factories, retail spaces and offices adding storage without a full build programme.

Learn more

Mezzanine Floor Offices

Raised offices and storage areas that maximise capacity while minimising footprint, adding room for collaboration and privacy. Also used for raised production areas and clean rooms.

Learn more

Warehouse Fit-outs

The whole space, not just the structure: walls and partitioning, electrical, data and lighting, mezzanine floors, floor coverings, shelving and pallet storage, painting, permit documentation, plumbing and line marking.

FAQ

What are the main benefits of installing a mezzanine floor?

Mezzanine floors let businesses use unused vertical space for extra storage or office areas instead of relocating. Dynamic’s mezzanines provide engineered designs that meet all Australian building standards, offering new storage and office space without changing your warehouse footprint.

Do your mezzanine floors comply with Australian standards?

Yes. Dynamic’s mezzanines come with full engineering certification and are designed to meet Australian Standards and building codes, ensuring safety and compliance.

What load ratings are available for mezzanine floors?

The company’s modular mezzanine system offers load ratings of 3 kPa (≈300 kg/m²) and 5 kPa (≈500 kg/m²). For custom solutions, Dynamic can accommodate any load rating required

Do mezzanine floors need footings or foundations?

No. Dynamic uses prefabricated designs that typically don’t require additional foundations; this allows faster installation and less disruption.

Do we need permits to install a mezzanine?

Building permits are usually required. Dynamic offers a complete planning and building‑permit management service so customers meet all regulatory requirements.

Do I need a site visit to get a quote?

Not initially. We can prepare a preliminary proposal and quote using drawings, photos, or rough sketches of your space. However, we recommend a free site visit to ensure the design matches your workflow, and a final site inspection is mandatory before fabrication.

What roof height is required for a mezzanine floor?

To comply with building codes, you need a minimum headroom clearance of 2100mm both below and above the mezzanine floor. Since the floor structure itself takes up a minimum of 250mm of vertical space, you will generally require a total roof height of 4450mm or more.

How long does installation take?

Installation is fast due to our pre-engineered, prefabricated components. Our experienced crews typically install between 50 and 100 square metres per day, meaning most mezzanine projects are completed within a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on the complexity.
